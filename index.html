Modelproof

Which AI model should you actually use? A calm decision tool — tell it your task, or the lab you already pay for, and get a straight answer. Not a benchmark race; the point is the decision.

  • Two ways in. Pick by task — what you're doing, and how you weigh cost vs. quality — or by lab, if you're already locked into one. You get a top pick plus runners-up, in plain English.
  • Sourced, never guessed. Pricing is verified against vendor pages, every benchmark carries a confidence flag, and a blank means "not reliably sourced" — the same discipline as DATproof.
  • Independent. Not sponsored by, or advertising for, any lab — models are ranked on merit and cost only.

DATproof

The rating agency for bitcoin-treasury proof. Ten public companies say they hold over a million BTC; DATproof tracks how much of that anyone can actually prove — and grades the evidence the way an auditor would.

  • Grades, not vibes. Every major corporate holder is scored on the evidence behind its coins — disclosure quality, staleness, attestations, leverage. A–F letter grades ship next, methodology public so anyone can re-run the math.
  • Pro-adoption, not gotcha. The thesis: provable, well-managed bitcoin on the balance sheet is treasury done right. A high grade is a badge — and today, the A column is empty.
  • It runs itself. A GitHub Action re-checks the chain and publishes a fresh brief every morning — 19 straight mornings and counting, no hands.

Skillproof

🧪 In the lab

The rating agency for Claude skills. Thousands of community skills exist; directories count them, nobody grades them. Skillproof installs each one, probes it in real sessions, and grades it A–F against a published rubric — with a filled worksheet behind every grade.

  • Receipts, not stars. Five graded dimensions, five trigger probes in fresh sessions, the skill's headline job run against a control — and a validator that re-derives every grade and fails the build on any mismatch.
  • No placeholder grades, ever. The index starts small because every entry costs a real grading run. First seeds graded July 2026; target: 25.

Warden

rls-guard-mcp 📦 npm launch imminent

A ship-readiness check for your Supabase backend. Point it at your own project and it tells you, in plain language, which tables anyone on the internet can already read or write — then hands you the exact SQL to lock each one down. No dashboard, no sign-up; your AI assistant runs it over MCP.

  • Severity that doesn't cry wolf. A table that's meant to be public reads as public-by-design; a table leaking emails reads as critical. Weighted by what's actually exposed.
  • Built from real work. It knows an accidentally-public table from a deliberately-public one — straight out of the Supabase RLS I wrote by hand for Prompt Emporium.
  • 19/19 tests green on Node's built-in test runner — run before every publish.
